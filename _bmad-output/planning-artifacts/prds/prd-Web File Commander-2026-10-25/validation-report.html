Decision-readiness

adequate

Decisions are stated as decisions (A1-A5 approved, FR-6 added). Open Questions 1-3 carry defaults, owners or deferral points, so they are genuinely open rather than rhetorical. Nothing blocks UX or architecture.

Substance over theater

strong

Two named journeys that each drive a requirement (UJ-1 drives FR-1/2/3/6, UJ-2 drives FR-5). No innovation or NFR theater. The Vision is generic in places ("calmly") but is short.

low

Vision wording is soft

§1
"browsing a folder clearly and calmly" could fit any browser.
Fix: Replace with the concrete promise: the user always knows whether the folder is loading, empty, blocked, or shown.

Strategic coherence

adequate

The thesis (a read-only browser that always explains its state) is stated and the five features follow from it. SM-1 maps to the acceptance tests. The counter-metric is vague.

low

Counter-metric not measurable

§7 SM-C1
"Number of features" has no threshold.
Fix: State it as: no write operations (edit, delete, move, upload) or preview exist in v2.

Done-ness clarity

adequate

Every FR has testable consequences, and FR-2, FR-4 and FR-5 quote the requirements tests. Gaps are an either/or outcome in FR-6 and an unmeasurable SM-2.

medium

FR-6 allows two outcomes at the top level

FR-6 third bullet
"not offered or has no effect" gives two different pass conditions, so a test cannot be written.
Fix: Pick one (default: go-back is not offered), and align it with Open Question 1.
medium

SM-2 is not measurable

§7 SM-2
"without any explanation" has no observer, task or pass criterion, even though A5 accepts informality.
Fix: Define a simple check: product owner completes select, open subfolder, go back in one attempt, unaided.
medium

Access denial covers only a user action

FR-5, UJ-2
"If the user denies access" leaves out access that is refused for any other reason (for example system permissions). The product behaviour should be the same.
Fix: Reword to "If access to a Folder is refused, for any reason" and keep the user-denial case as the tested example.

Scope honesty

strong

Non-Goals, MVP in/out, per-FR out-of-scope notes, and an approved Assumptions Index make omissions explicit. Open-item count (3 questions, 5 assumptions) is proportionate for a hobby PRD.

Downstream usability

adequate

Glossary exists and FR, UJ and SM IDs resolve. Small drift and ordering issues remain (see mechanical notes). A story author can extract FR-1 to FR-6 cleanly.

low

Glossary terms used in lowercase

§2.3 UJ-1/UJ-2, FR-2
"folder", "subfolder", "loading indicator" and "access-denied message" appear in lowercase prose, and "subfolder" is not a Glossary term.
Fix: Capitalise glossary terms in journeys, and add Subfolder to the Glossary or define it under Entry.

Shape fit

strong

A short capability-spec shape with two light journeys suits a hobby, single-purpose app.
